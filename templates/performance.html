What is CV RMSE?

Average RMSE across 5 validation folds during tuning. Lower means better generalisation.

Why 5-fold CV?

Uses all training data for validation while preventing overfitting to any single split.

CV vs Test RMSE

CV RMSE comes from training data only. Test RMSE is the final honest evaluation on unseen data.
